**Free Degree Library** is a modern, student-powered academic platform designed for Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) students to easily upload, discover, and share study materials, notes, assignments, and Previous Year Questions (PYQs) completely free of charge.

---

## ✨ Features & Sections

- 🏠 **Home & Material Repository:** Fast search and filtering by custom tags, subjects, and categories (Computers, Business/Commerce, Science, Law).
- 📤 **Community Uploads:** Easily share valuable class notes, lecture summaries, or exam prep guides directly from any device.
- 💬 **Interactive Community Board:** A real-time discussion forum where students can ask doubts, post solutions, and vote in community polls.
- 🎓 **MCA Dedicated Hub:** Complete specialized semester notes and exam prep papers (including SV University MCA special collections).
- 📑 **ICET PYQ Center:** Extensive archive of ICET Previous Year Question papers broken down by shift and exam year.
- 📢 **Academic Alerts & Newsletter:** Custom notification email triggers and push alerts for new uploads, polls, and announcements.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** Vanilla HTML5, Custom Design System with responsive vanilla CSS (`style.css`), and modular JavaScript (`app.js`).
- **Backend:** PHP API endpoints for file uploads, community discussions, email notification distribution, and database operations.
- **Database:** MySQL / MariaDB relational structure for managing uploads, community posts, comments, likes, and subscribers.
- **Email Delivery:** PHPMailer & Custom Mail Helper routing for real-time newsletters and subscriber verification.
